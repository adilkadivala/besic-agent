## 📜 The Article Document (30 Deep Dives)

*Articles should be posted as X Threads or long-form "Articles" (Premium feature). Here is the conceptual outline for each day.*

### Week 1: RAG & Engineering
- **Day 1**: **RAG Demystified**. Explain the pipeline: Indexing $\rightarrow$ Retrieval $\rightarrow$ Generation. Discuss the "Naive RAG" vs. "Advanced RAG" (Query transformation, Re-ranking).
- **Day 2**: **Multi-Tenant RAG**. How to ensure User A never sees User B's data. Discuss metadata filtering and isolated namespaces.
- **Day 3**: **The RAG Eval Crisis**. Why "it looks right" isn't a metric. Introduce the RAG Triad: Faithfulness, Answer Relevance, and Context Relevance.
- **Day 4**: **The Invisible Pipeline**. Deep dive into PDF parsing, OCR, and cleaning. Why `pdftotext` isn't enough for production.
- **Day 5**: **AI System Design**. The core patterns: The Router pattern, the Orchestrator pattern, and the Memory pattern.
- **Day 6**: **LLM Ops 101**. Monitoring drift, managing latency, and the importance of a "Golden Dataset" for regression testing.
- **Day 7**: **Recap**. Synthesize the week into a "RAG Maturity Model".

### Week 2: Agentic AI
- **Day 8**: **Agentic AI vs. Chatbots**. The fundamental difference: Tool use, planning, and loop execution.
- **Day 9**: **The Autonomy Loop**. Perception $\rightarrow$ Planning $\rightarrow$ Action $\rightarrow$ Reflection. How agents "think" before they act.
- **Day 10**: **Orchestration Battle**. AutoGen (Conversational) vs. CrewAI (Role-based). When to use which?
- **Day 11**: **Agent Architecture**. Designing for reliability: The "Supervisor" pattern and the "Worker" pattern.
- **Day 12**: **Recursive MAS**. Exploring "Latent Thoughts"—how agents can share internal representations instead of just text.
- **Day 13**: **Scaling Agents**. The challenges of agent-to-agent communication: Token explosions and "infinite loops".
- **Day 14**: **The Agency Era**. Why the future of software is not a UI, but a fleet of specialized agents.

### Week 3: Reliability & Workflows
- **Day 15**: **Durable Execution**. Why `timeout` is the enemy. Discuss the "Virtual Actor" model and how to make agents survive restarts.
- **Day 16**: **Stateful AI**. Why agents need a "memory" that isn't just a chat history. The role of external state stores.
- **Day 17**: **Loop Engineering**. Breaking down the `while` loop of AI. How to implement "exit conditions" so agents don't burn your budget.
- **Day 18**: **The AI Harness**. Creating a "test harness" for agents. How to simulate environments to verify agent behavior.
- **Day 19**: **Context Window Hacks**. Sliding windows, summary-buffers, and the "lost in the middle" phenomenon.
- **Day 20**: **The Voice Frontier**. Latency challenges in Agentic Voice AI. The move toward end-to-end neural voice agents.
- **Day 21**: **The Reliability Moat**. Why the winner in AI isn't the one with the best model, but the one with the most reliable system.

### Week 4: Mastery & Career
- **Day 22**: **Transformer Deep Dive**. Attention mechanisms explained simply. Why the "Attention" paper changed everything.
- **Day 23**: **The Tuning Guide**. LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation) explained. When to fine-tune vs. when to use RAG.
- **Day 24**: **Coding Copilots**. Beyond autocomplete: Repository-level context and the challenge of "Correctness".
- **Day 25**: **Physical AI**. NVIDIA Cosmos 3 and the bridge between LLMs and the physical world (Robotics).
- **Day 26**: **The Death of Prompt Engineering**. Why "coding the system" is the new "writing the prompt". The shift to AI Engineering.
- **Day 27**: **Interview Secrets**. How to approach a "Design an AI System" question. Start with requirements $\rightarrow$ data flow $\rightarrow$ model choice $\rightarrow$ eval.
- **Day 28**: **The 100 Patterns**. A curated list of the most common AI design patterns used in Big Tech (e.g., Speculative Decoding, Mixture of Experts).
- **Day 29**: **The FDE Role**. What a Forward Deployed Engineer does and why it's the fastest way to learn AI in the real world.
- **Day 30**: **The 2026 Roadmap**. A step-by-step guide to becoming a top 1% AI Engineer.
