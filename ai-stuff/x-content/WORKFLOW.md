# Notes → X Content Workflow

Turn daily study notes into posts and articles without starting from a blank page.

**Your stage:** Learning-in-public (Phase 0–1: vocab, math, deep learning foundations)  
**Tone:** Builder learning out loud — honest mistakes, plain English, no fake expert voice  
**Link:** Use `X_Monetization_Content_Plan.md` for *future* advanced topics (RAG/agents) once you study them. Until then, post from **real notes only**.

---

## Why this works better than faking expertise

| Fake expert | Learning in public (you) |
|-------------|--------------------------|
| “Here’s how production RAG works” (never built it) | “I thought hallucination = data leak. Wrong.” |
| Easy to get called out | Relatable; people save beginner explainers |
| Hard to keep up | Notes already exist → copy-edit → post |

**Rule:** Only post concepts you can explain in your own words from `notes/`.

## Content pillars (mix each week)

| Pillar | % | What it is | Example |
|--------|---|------------|---------|
| **Learn** | 40% | One concept, plain English | “What is a token?” |
| **Mistake** | 20% | Wrong answer + corrected version | Hallucination confusion (Day 1) |
| **Math → DL** | 15% | Why this math shows up in nets | Dot product = neuron combine |
| **Progress** | 15% | Roadmap check-ins | “Day 3: backprop intuition ✓” |
| **Question** | 10% | Ask followers | “How did you first understand gradients?” |

Advanced topics (RAG, agents, multi-tenant) come later from the 30-day plan — after you’ve studied those PDFs.

## Daily cadence (sustainable)

Start **light** so you don’t burn out next to studying:

| Cadence | Posts/day | Articles | Time |
|---------|-----------|----------|------|
| **Start (now)** | 1–2 | 1 thread/week | ~20 min |
| **Grow** | 3 | 2–3 threads/week | ~40 min |
| **Full plan** | 5 + article | Daily article | 1–2 hr (later) |

**Suggested day structure:**
1. **Morning:** 1 short post (hook + one idea)
2. **Evening (optional):** progress post or community question
3. **Weekend:** 1 long thread or X Article from the week’s notes

---

## 10-minute pipeline (after each study day)

```
notes/day-XX.md
    ↓  pick 1 “content atom”
    ↓  write hook (first line)
    ↓  3–6 short lines, plain English
    ↓  optional CTA (bookmark / reply)
    ↓  post or save in ready-to-post/
```

### What counts as a “content atom”?

- A definition you rewrote in your own words  
- A wrong answer + teacher fix (gold for engagement)  
- A formula + one-line meaning (`z = w·x + b`)  
- A score / checkpoint (“Avg 6.7/10 — back tomorrow”)  
- A comparison table (chatbot vs agent, backprop vs GD)

### Hook formulas (use one)

- “I was wrong about ___.”  
- “In plain English: ___ is just ___.”  
- “Day N of learning AI from zero:”  
- “Stop confusing ___ and ___.”  
- “One formula. Everything in deep learning builds on this:”

### Hashtags (1–3 max)

`#AI` `#BuildInPublic` `#DeepLearning` `#MachineLearning` `#LearnInPublic`

Avoid stuffing 8 hashtags — looks spammy on X.

---

## Formats

### 1) Single post (~150–280 chars work great)
Hook → insight → optional question.

### 2) Thread (5–10 tweets)
1 hook → 2–8 value tweets → recap + “follow for Day N+1”

### 3) X Article (long-form Premium)
Title + short intro + sections from notes + “what I got wrong” + next study step.

### 4) Progress post
Day number + topics + 1 takeaway + 1 open question.

---

## Quality bar (before you hit Post)

- [ ] Explained in **your** words (not copied PDF jargon)  
- [ ] First line works alone as a hook  
- [ ] No claim you haven’t studied yet  
- [ ] One clear idea per post  
- [ ] Mistake posts include the **correct** version
